## 1. Notation and safe-ancestor coverage theorem — DG-004-A (PROVED)

Let `T(x)=x/2` for even `x`, `T(x)=(3x+1)/2` for odd `x`; `E={n>=2: T^j(n)>=n for all j>=1}`. For a parity word `w` of length `L`, with `s` odd steps, the specified branch is
[
T^L(z)=(3^s z+q_w)/2^L,qquad q_wge0.
]
Call `w` **strictly prefix-expanding** when `3^{s_j}>2^j` for every `1<=j<=L`. Necessarily `A=3^s>D=2^L`.

**Theorem.** For any such word `w`, put `r_w=D^{-1}q_wpmod A` (inverse taken modulo `A`). Every positive integer `y` with
[
yequiv r_wpmod A,qquad z=(Dy-q_w)/Age2
]
has an *actual* positive-integer ancestor `2<=z<y` following exactly `w`, with `T^j(z)>z` for all `1<=j<=L` and `T^L(z)=y`. Consequently, if `y∈E`, then `z∈E`; hence `y` cannot be `min E`.

**Proof.** The stated congruence makes `z` an integer, and `Az+q_w=Dy` shows `Az+q_wequiv0pmod D`. Since `A` is odd, this is exactly the single residue `zpmod{2^L}` of word `w`: compare any realised `w`-prefix with the affine identity, then use invertibility of `A` modulo `D` and DG001's parity-cylinder bijection. Thus the *actual* `T` trajectory from `z` follows `w`. At each prefix `j`, `T^j(z)=(3^{s_j}z+q_j)/2^j>z`, because `q_j>=0` and `3^{s_j}>2^j`. Also `z=(Dy-q_w)/A<y` since `D<A`, `y>0`, `q_w>=0`. DG003's safe-ancestor transfer then gives `z∈E` whenever `y∈E`, contradicting least-exception minimality if `y=min E`. QED. **Scope:** a theorem about the *least* exceptional integer; not a deletion of these classes from all of `E`.

## 4. Third avenue: beyond parity cylinders, and attempted whole-E exclusions

The suffix-minimum spacing theorem is a genuine **infinite-horizon, positive-orbit-dependent** condition: it compares widely separated *actual orbit values*, not just parity-cylinder residues. It rules out arbitrarily fast-growing sequences as complete tail-record lists of a hypothetical unbounded exceptional orbit. But the sparse countermodel above defeats an argument for positive density using just spacing, parity, and integrality. A genuine Collatz-specific correlation among different record minima remains missing.

The deep inverse sieve (§§1–2) yields new infinite arithmetic families forbidden for **the least member of E**. It does *not* prove that `E` misses any new full congruence family: a nonleast exceptional endpoint could have a smaller exceptional ancestor. Nor does a finite parity itinerary give a universal descent result. In particular, DG001's `2^kt-1` and DG002's `8^t-5` survive arbitrarily long initial intervals; no proposed uniform time cap is valid. No new all-E excluded family with genuinely unbounded first-descent horizons was proved in this session.

Multiples of three remain immune to **all** backward shrinking certificates: every inverse ancestor of such a number is a larger doubling (DG003-D), irrespective of word length. Thus the sieve cannot reach `m≡0 mod3`. This is not evidence such an `m` exists. Also a finite-horizon example such as `3→5→8→4` illustrates why one cannot automatically transfer any survivor property forward to `T(n)`; it is **not** a counterexample to an assertion about actual `E` members, whose existence is unknown.
