(A_0,q_0,D_0)=(1,0,1),
\]
where appending 1 maps \((A,q,D)\) to \((3A,3q+D,2D)\), and 0 maps it to \((A,q,2D)\). Let \((A,q,D)=(A_d,q_d,2^d)\).

\[
r_w\equiv qD^{-1}\pmod A,\qquad z=(Dm-q)/A.
\]
For any odd \(m\equiv r_w\pmod A\) with \(z\ge3\), the integer \(z\) follows exactly \(w\), all its first \(d\) iterates are \(\ge z\), and \(z<m\). If \(m\in E\), then \(z\in E\), contradicting minimality. To certify an *entire* odd class, check the least positive odd endpoint representative \(m_0\) and its \(z_0\ge3\). Increasing \(m\) by \(2A\) increases \(z\) by \(2D\), preserving odd parity, the parity cylinder, and \(z<m\).

## DG-008-C: third additive off-spine inverse-root family (PROVED, conditional count)

**Universal safe-predecessor lemma.** For any odd \(b\ge91\) with \(b\equiv10\bmod81\), write \(b=91+162k\), \(k\ge0\), and
\[
z=(64b-73)/81=71+128k,\quad 3\le z<b.
\]
There is an *actual* six-step word \(z\xrightarrow{111010}b\), with affine endpoint \((81z+73)/64=b\). Every prefix multiplier \(3^{s_i}/2^i\ge1\), hence its initial iterates remain \(\ge z\). Consequently **\(b\in E\Rightarrow z\in E\)**, irrespective of whether \(b\) belongs to a bounded or unbounded orbit.

**Off-spine record theorem.** For a genuine unbounded E-record target \(b=a_{j+1}\equiv10\bmod81\) after gap \(d_j\ge9\), \(z\) is not on the original injective orbit. Were it there, injectivity and \(T^6(z)=b\) force \(z=x_{t_{j+1}-6}\), strictly inside that record interval, where all values are \(>b\), contradicting \(z<b\). Its deterministic trajectory first meets the original orbit at some index in \((t_j,t_{j+1}]\), since its own index zero is off-spine and the remaining six steps end at \(b\). Different record intervals have disjoint first-intersection times, so these roots are pairwise distinct; within each record interval the three endpoint classes \(2\bmod3,\ 4\bmod9,\ 10\bmod81\) are mutually exclusive, proving disjointness also from DG006/DG007's previously counted roots.

Define
\[
Q_{10}(X)=\#\{j:a_{j+1}\le X,\ d_j\ge9,\ a_{j+1}\equiv10\pmod{81}\}.
\]
With DG007's definitions of \(R(X)\) and \(Q_4(X)\), the strengthened conditional count is
\[

+\sum_{\substack{j:a_{j+1}\le X\\d_j\ge3,\ a_{j+1}\equiv2\pmod3}}v_3(a_{j+1}+1)
+Q_4(X)+Q_{10}(X),\quad X\ge n.}

For gap 6, \(111010\) has \(z=a_j\), so **do not count it anew**. In the actual positive *finite* gap-9 example \(63\xrightarrow{111111000}91\), the distinct positive root \(71\xrightarrow{111010}91\) is off the nine-step spine; this verifies local geometry but does not assert any member of E. Crucially there is no established lower frequency for \(Q_{10}\), \(Q_4\) or the \(2\bmod3\) terms.
