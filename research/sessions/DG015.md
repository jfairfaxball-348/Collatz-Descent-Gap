## 1. The new separated-repeat theorem (DG-015-A, PROVED)

For r >= 5, r = 5 (mod 6), define s(r) as the least positive s such that (3/2)^s > 2^r; the mountain word is w_r = 1^s 0^r, of binary length d(r) = s(r)+r >= 14. Its affine endpoint is

    F_r(x) = (3^s (x+1) - 2^s)/2^(s+r)
           = lambda_r*x + eta_r,
    lambda_r = 3^s/2^(s+r) in (1,3/2),
    eta_r = lambda_r - 2^(-r) in (0,3/2).

For any **actual positive integer** orbit following consecutive complete mountain parity words, write a_j for its start of block j. Then, for all j,

    a_(j+1) = F_(r_j)(a_j) > a_j,
    a_(j+1)+1 < 2(a_j+1),

the second inequality following for a_j>=1 from lambda_r<3/2 and eta_r<3/2: F_r(a)+1 < (3/2)a+5/2 <=2(a+1). Thus

    a_q+1 < 2^q(a_0+1)                  (q>=1).             (1)

**Separated-repeat lemma.** Suppose the mountain-parameter factor (r_p,...,r_(p+h-1)) equals (r_q,...,r_(q+h-1)), with p<q, h>=1, and let L be the total **binary** length of these h mountain words. If a *single ordinary positive integer* n=a_0 follows all blocks through q+h-1, then

    2^L | (a_q-a_p),   0<a_q-a_p<a_q+1<2^q(n+1),           (2)

and therefore

    2^(L-q)<n+1.                                           (3)

**Proof.** Both a_p and a_q follow the same parity prefix of L shortened-Collatz steps. DG001's exact parity-cylinder bijection says any length-L parity prefix has exactly one starting residue modulo 2^L, so a_p = a_q (mod 2^L). The mountain endpoint maps are strictly increasing in the block index, giving a_q>a_p. A positive multiple of 2^L is at least 2^L, and (1) supplies the upper bound. This uses an actual *fixed positive integer*, not 2-adic compactness. The congruence is the existing parity cylinder, not a new independent congruence. QED.

**Infinite obstruction:** if an infinite prescribed mountain schedule contains pairs of identical length-h_k parameter factors, with later starting indices q_k and binary lengths L_k satisfying L_k-q_k -> +infinity, **no positive integer realises it**. More quantitatively, any fixed positive itinerary must have L-q < log_2(n+1) for every separated equal-factor pair. Since d(r)>=14, it suffices that 14 h_k-q_k -> +infinity. Neither consecutive factor squares nor eventual monotonicity is assumed.

## 2. Bounded Thue schedule eliminated (DG-015-B, PROVED)

Let phi(0)=012, phi(1)=02, phi(2)=1, and u=phi^infinity(0) its fixed word (as defined in DG014). Define r_j by mapping 0,1,2 to 5,11,17. For k>=1, put H_k=|phi^k(0)|. An induction on the three substitution lengths gives

    |phi^k(0)|=3*2^(k-1), |phi^k(1)|=2^k, |phi^k(2)|=2^(k-1).

The prefix phi^(k+2)(0) begins with phi^(k+1)(0) phi^(k+1)(1). The first length-H_k block is phi^k(0). Since phi^(k+1)(1)=phi^k(0)phi^k(2) and |phi^(k+1)(0)|=2H_k, **another identical length-H_k factor begins at q_k=2H_k**, with the first starting at p=0.

The common mountain parity factor has length L_k>=14H_k; hence L_k-q_k>=12H_k -> infinity. Equation (3) would require n+1>2^(12H_k) for every k: impossible for fixed n. Thus **no ordinary positive integer** follows the entire bounded square-free Thue mountain schedule. This is independent of Thue's *classical imported square-freeness* theorem: only its substitution identity is used.

## 3. Sparse-spike schedule eliminated as well (DG-015-C, PROVED)

All positions of these two intervals lie **strictly between** consecutive spike indices

Consequently **no ordinary positive integer** follows the infinite DG014 sparse-spike schedule, despite all its finite prefixes having positive integer strict-bridge witnesses. The spikes need not be removed or assumed bounded. The argument does not contradict DG014's correct statement that neither abstract parameter sequence has **adjacent** repeated superwords: our repeated factors are *separated*.

## 4. Arbitrary E orbits and the least-exception/preperiodic gate (DG-015-D)

The proof gives an exact necessary high-complexity condition **conditional on being an infinite mountain itinerary**, not an unconditional restriction on E. There is no theorem implying that an arbitrary unbounded E orbit has any complete mountain-block suffix. Its record bridges can have arbitrary parity words and arbitrary gaps. The DG011 shield (expanding inverse to 7 mod9 requires >=7 odd steps and length >=11) and DG010 two-gap first-meeting windows remain valid, but this session proves **no** actual-orbit recurrence of divergent roots, no collision-free density-incompatible count, and no enhancement of E=o(X).

A modest unconditional *least-E orbit restriction* is available. **Lemma:** If E is nonempty and m=min(E), then m is odd, and for **every** visited value y=T^t(m), t>=1, with y=2 (mod 3),

    y >= (3m+1)/2 = T(m).                                 (4)

Proof: m even would descend at once; hence m>=3 is odd. Every visited y>=m, so a visited y=2(mod3) must satisfy y>=5 and its positive odd inverse z=(2y-1)/3 is at least 3, with T(z)=y. If z<m, all subsequent iterates of z are points of m's orbit, hence at least m>z, so z∈E contrary to minimality. Thus z>=m and (4). Equality occurs at y=T(m), so the bound is sharp and is **not** a contradiction.

If m is a hypothetical 3-divisible least preperiodic E seed entering an unknown nontrivial cycle at y, then DG014 implies y=2 (mod 3), and (4) gives y>=T(m) (strict if its first cycle entry is later than t=1, since equality would revisit T(m) already on the cycle). This does **not** eliminate such a cycle or its preperiodic m. All actual visits to 2 (mod 3), not only certified E-records, obey (4), but the analogous implication for arbitrary numbers outside the fixed E orbit is invalid.
