## 4. Conditional two-gap first-meeting and disjoint-root theorem

Let \(a_j=x_{t_j}\) be increasing actual suffix minima of **one fixed positive unbounded E orbit**, \(d_j=t_{j+1}-t_j\). Suppose \(j\ge1\), there is a strict-safe positive root \(2\le z_j<a_j\) with \(T^{\ell_j}(z_j)=a_{j+1}\), and
\[
\ell_j<d_{j-1}+d_j.
\]
Then \(z_j\in E\), lies **off** the original orbit, and first meets it in the two-gap time interval \((t_{j-1},t_{j+1}]\). Proof: an unbounded deterministic orbit is injective. If \(z_j=x_t\), its \(\ell_j\)-step endpoint forces \(t=t_{j+1}-\ell_j>t_{j-1}\), but every original orbit value after \(t_{j-1}\) is at least \(a_j\) (strictly greater inside the preceding gap), contradicting \(z_j<a_j\). Similarly any meeting \(T^p(z_j)=x_t\) forces \(t=t_{j+1}-\ell_j+p>t_{j-1}\).

\[
|E\cap[2,X]|\ge R(X)+\lceil|J(X)|/2\rceil,\qquad R(X)=\#\{j:a_j\le X\}.
\]
