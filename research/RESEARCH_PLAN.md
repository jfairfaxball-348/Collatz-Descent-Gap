# Research programme: almost all → all

Single headline problem: **Terras–Everett finite descent for almost all → finite descent for every n>1**.

Research path (not independent projects):
1. Build exact prefix algebra and parity-cylinder survival classification. DG001 records the baseline; its results are classical and should not be represented as novel.
2. Extract arithmetic compatibility conditions for a *fixed positive integer* surviving all prefixes, carefully distinguishing congruence classes, 2-adic infinite words and integer realisation.
3. Attempt and falsify concrete elimination lemmas for classes of permanent survivors. A failed candidate should produce a counterexample or precise gap, not bureaucratic audits.
4. Search for a non-circular bridge converting Terras–Everett density-zero into E empty using Collatz-specific dynamics; compare to Tao's logarithmic-density result only where logically helpful.
5. Verify significant results independently; formalise worthwhile proved statements (Lean if appropriate) after scope and mathematical correctness are established.
