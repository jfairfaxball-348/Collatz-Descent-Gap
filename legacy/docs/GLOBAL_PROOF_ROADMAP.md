# GLOBAL PROOF ROADMAP

Status: PERMANENT RESEARCH-PROCESS PROTOCOL
Introduced: RL324 closeout, 2026-09-14

## Purpose

Every authoritative research closeout must carry a compact high-level navigation layer showing exactly where the project stands on the logical route from the current research frontier to a proof that positive non-trivial Collatz cycles cannot exist.

This roadmap supplements theorem-grade handover material. It never replaces proof ledgers, scope qualifications, correction/demotion records, barriers, verifiers, or detailed successor targets.

The stages are logical stages, not claims that later stages are solved, equally difficult, or guaranteed to proceed linearly without strategic audit.

## Fixed stages

- **R1 — Parent Bridge** Convert the current genuine ownership / crossing machinery into contradiction, genuine owned descent, an absolute canonical-interface bound, or an equivalent parent-level closure theorem.

- **R2 — g=2 Closure** Use the R1 theorem to eliminate every surviving genuine `g=2` branch, not merely the current canonical subbranch.

- **R3 — g>1 Lift** Lift the `g=2` closure through the established general `g>1` reduction/descent architecture so no `g>1` positive non-trivial cycle can survive.

- **R4 — Scale Closure** Remove or exactly cover all internal/external frontier gaps, conditional state-floor dependencies, first-survivor restrictions, and finite residual ranges needed for the global theorem.

- **R5 — g=1 Closure** Close the separate `g=1` case completely.

- **R6 — Gate Reconciliation** Re-audit Gate A, Gate B, encounter/ownership obligations, and historical branch structure under the new theorems; prove whatever genuinely remains and explicitly retire obligations that have become redundant.

- **R7 — End-to-End Proof** Assemble and red-team the full proof from the assumption of a positive non-trivial cycle through minimality/reduction, all branch closures, and final contradiction.

## Mandatory closeout report

Every authoritative RL closeout must include a compact section headed exactly:

`GLOBAL PROOF ROADMAP STATUS`

It must state:

1. **CURRENT_STAGE** — one of `R1` through `R7`.
2. **CURRENT_STAGE_NAME**.
3. **CURRENT_STAGE_PROGRESS** — a coarse approximate percentage or qualitative fraction based on theorem-level obligations completed, not sessions or effort.
4. **THIS_SESSION_ADVANCE** — one or two sentences naming the exact current-stage obligation removed, reduced, corrected, or newly exposed.
5. **CURRENT_STAGE_BLOCKER** — the smallest remaining theorem/bridge preventing advancement.
6. **ADVANCE_CRITERION** — an explicit theorem-level condition justifying transition to the next roadmap stage.
7. **REMAINING_STAGES** — every later stage still genuinely open.
8. **ROADMAP_DELTA** — exactly one of:
   - `MAJOR_ADVANCE`
   - `ADVANCE`
   - `LATERAL`
   - `REGRESSION/CORRECTION`
9. **GLOBAL_PROOF_STATUS** — `OPEN` unless the complete end-to-end proof has actually been established and audited.

The authoritative successor handover must inherit the current roadmap state.

## Progress-percentage discipline

Do not infer progress from time spent, RL session count, algebra volume, code volume, or subjective optimism.

Estimate from the fraction and importance of theorem-level obligations actually discharged.

A large local calculation that does not reduce the current-stage blocker should not materially increase the estimate. A theorem removing the principal blocker may justify a large jump. A correction, demotion, or newly discovered necessary obligation may reduce the estimate.

Prefer coarse values such as `25%`, `50%`, `70%`, or `90%`; false precision is prohibited.

## Transition discipline

Do not advance stages merely because substantial progress occurred.

- R1 does not close merely because another crossing identity or support bound is found.
- R2 does not close until every surviving `g=2` branch is eliminated or subsumed.
- R3 does not close until the general `g>1` reduction is proved to terminate in contradiction/descent.
- R4 does not close while any proof-critical scale interval or external conditional assumption remains uncovered.
- R5 remains separate until `g=1` is genuinely closed.
- R6 requires an explicit audit of Gate A, Gate B, and the global architecture rather than assuming they disappeared automatically.
- R7 requires a complete end-to-end proof audit.

If a strategic audit proves that a later-stage-looking obligation is actually an upstream dependency of the current stage, record that explicitly without pretending the roadmap stage has advanced.

## Successor-session rule

Every successor handover must instruct the next session to work on the **smallest theorem that advances the current GLOBAL PROOF ROADMAP stage**, unless a strategic audit explicitly shows that another stage contains an upstream dependency that must be resolved first.

Local mathematical momentum does not override this rule.
