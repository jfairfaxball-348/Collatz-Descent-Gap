# FINAL_CHANCE verdict

## STOP — NOT VIABLE

Date: 2026-09-18

The bounded six-session FINAL_CHANCE programme is closed.

This verdict is about the **Bridge Problem defined in FINAL_CHANCE/README.md**:
whether the project's already-proved local, finite, branch-restricted, phase,
radius, defect, and ownership-reformulation machinery supplies a defensible
route to an unrestricted theorem.  It is not a claim that the Collatz
conjecture is false, not a proof that no future mathematics can solve the
remaining branch, and not a demotion of the valid local theorems.

### Why the bridge programme is not viable

Across the six sessions, every natural route for promoting the inherited
machinery to the arbitrary surviving object either collapsed to the original
ownership condition or was broken by an explicit structural countermodel.

The decisive final point is algebraic.  For a fixed positive accelerated word,

D m = Q, where D=2^A-3^L.

Consequently, requiring the global middle orbit to close on integer physical
states is exactly the condition D|Q.  The historical RL167 propagation theorem
then makes every cyclic phase integral automatically.  There is no weaker
"middle physical closure" lemma sitting between the existing structural data
and full ownership: once integrality is required, one has simply restated the
open ownership problem.

Session 6 attacks the strongest remaining version from below.  The profile

h_j=1 for 2<=j<=L-1, with h_0=h_1=h_L=0,

has exact inherited counts, positive exponents, nonnegative defect, defect
area 137528045310, boundary collar p+(L-q)=3, a strict least root inside
[2^71,2^75), and an exact full-period ordinary +1 affine recurrence through
the entire middle.  Its unique closing root is nevertheless non-integral.
Thus full global affine dynamics plus the strongest structural constraints
obtained in FINAL_CHANCE still do not force ordinary ownership.

Earlier sessions eliminate the obvious alternate routes:

- local/radius and defect grammar do not force the arbitrary branch;
- area two can be eliminated globally, but the Session 3 sparse-collapse
  mechanism cannot be made height/area independent;
- residue-order sparse/degree/Parseval control is structurally killed;
- endpoint lifts and state-window bounds do not force ownership;
- exact physical prefix and suffix data can select the same candidate least
  state while the global middle remains unconstrained;
- bare resultants, distinguished-root elimination/SNF, and repeated all-phase
  affine closure are reformulations of the same ownership datum rather than
  independent consumers.

A useful positive reduction remains: the Session 5 boundary-cylinder work
reduces any surviving owner, subject to its stated verification status, to
p+(L-q)<=37 and 744 boundary signatures.  But those signatures still contain
arbitrary middle height, area, component count, and run structure.  Treating
them as a finite list of complete objects would recreate the same
local-to-global error this programme was designed to detect.

### Final scope

The programme therefore stops with the following exact frontier:

> Full ordinary ownership D|Q_h is the unresolved arithmetic condition.
> The existing project machinery does not provide a non-circular bridge that
> forces or excludes it for arbitrary surviving profiles.

Further work could only be justified by a genuinely new ownership theorem or
new arithmetic input that attacks D|Q_h directly.  Repackaging the existing
local constraints, endpoint data, phase polynomials, resultants, state-window
bounds, or finite boundary signatures would not constitute such a bridge.

Cumulative strikes at closure: **2/3**.

The STOP verdict is due to the six-session attack and the resulting structural
barrier, not to the mechanical three-strike rule.

No RL-numbered session, Phase, Gate, or successor research architecture is
opened by this verdict.
