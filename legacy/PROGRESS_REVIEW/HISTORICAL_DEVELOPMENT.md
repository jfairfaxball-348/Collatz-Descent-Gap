# Historical development through RL349

## Foundations and repair (early sessions through RL20)

The project developed parity-word, affine, denominator, rotation, and ownership language. RL18 repaired a radius-3 proof chain and extracted an arbitrary-radius orbit-sum identity. RL19 recorded a closed local radius-3 obstruction under inherited hypotheses but also stated that no theorem forced a global RL object into radius 3. RL20 supplied an exact non-cycle word satisfying the extracted local grammar while every cyclic rotation pair avoided radius 3. The durable lesson was that a local obstruction needs a genuine global bridge.

## Gate architecture and global audits (RL43–RL79)

The Gate-A line developed valuation/rank/full-phase and ownership machinery; Gate B retained the full-denominator/close-rotation/ownership route. RL72, RL75, and RL79 consolidated the route genealogy and exposed recurring barriers: raw local grammar, coboundary identities, normalized homogeneous invariants, and generalized-increment-insensitive arguments do not by themselves distinguish ordinary `+1` ownership. RL79 made the `s=1` discriminator explicit.

## Physical and branch-sieve era (RL80–RL255)

The blue/dyadic basin route was tested and shown too weak as a set-theoretic trap. Subsequent work introduced physical lifts, Farey/product tubes, synchronized differences, reset budgets, 2-adic rectangles, multiscale covers, and branch-specific capacity/packing. The RL99 audit and RL314 audit both preserved the central scope warning: stronger finite or branch-level sieves do not become global without an exhaustive branch-to-global theorem. RL255 is retained as an example of a genuine Branch-C capacity result that explicitly does not close Gate A/B.

## Parent-bridge reset (RL309–RL342)

The programme increasingly targeted an ownership-sensitive parent-level contradiction or descent. RL309 identified the recurring missing resource as an all-scale global physical reachability/ownership constraint. RL324 formalised the R1–R7 roadmap. RL329–RL342 produced increasingly strong exact physical, density, phase, and recurrence contractions while preserving R1 OPEN and the conditional least-state floor.

## Phase 4 residual (RL343–RL349)

RL343 promoted the full physical two-row bridge, a terminal 60-gap escape certificate, and the definition of the exact residual `O_75`. RL344 converted endpoint/profile enumeration into record and valuation reductions and a singleton-interface theorem. RL345–RL346 contracted 23/72 interfaces and bounded cyclic signatures. RL347 added decorated rank uniqueness, strict-late geometry, high-carry exclusion, and the matched contact-pair contraction. RL348 added the terminal gap/tag law and a strict-late complement interval for over-half returns. RL349 did not eliminate a residual class; it localized the missing orientation/sign bridge, recorded scope barriers, and demoted live numerics that depended on that bridge.

The current frontier is therefore a logical residual, not simply an unrun computation.
