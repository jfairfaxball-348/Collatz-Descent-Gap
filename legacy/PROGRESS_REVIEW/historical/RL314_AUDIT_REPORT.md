## 5. RL314 audit-derived spacing splice and correction

A valid cross-era observation was derived during the audit.

For a terminal equal-row decomposition with counts `(d,ell0)`, let
`X=2^d`, `Y=3^ell0`, `D0=X-Y`, and let `qmax-qmin` denote the row-numerator
diameter.

If two inactive row cuts are separated by a common parity block of length `H`
containing `r` ones, then for any two row-boundary states `x_k,x_l` and their
states after the common block `x'_k,x'_l`,

`2^H (x'_k-x'_l)=3^r (x_k-x_l)`.

Hence
`2^H | (x_k-x_l)` and `3^r | (x'_k-x'_l)`.

Using the RL313 terminal row-state interval at both inactive cuts gives the
necessary spacing inequality

`(n-1) max(2^H,3^r) D0 <= qmax-qmin`.

This is a valid physical multi-occupancy/congruence-fibre splice.

However its strategic leverage was initially overstated in scratch.
At `(d,ell0)=(184,116)`, one must also enforce the terminal condition
`(2^d/3^ell0)^n<3`; therefore an illustrative `n=10000` example was invalid.
In fact the allowable `n` there is small.

More generally, after combining the spacing inequality with `lambda<3`, the
result does not uniformly improve RL313's universal support lower bound beyond
the already-proved exclusion of support 0, 1, and 2.

Historical comparison also shows this mechanism is an application of the
already-known physical multi-occupancy packing principle emphasized in RL251,
not a wholly new project-level resource.

Classification:
`VALID LOCAL/CROSS-ERA LEMMA; PARENT_DELTA = LATERAL`.

Do not promote the earlier overstatement that it forces support to grow
unboundedly with arbitrary `n`.

## 6. Why direct support-3 continuation is not recommended

RL313's two-active proof uses a decisive small-coefficient condition: after
normalization the coefficient diameter is `<X=2^d`, allowing successive
modulo-X annihilation.

For three active cuts, natural row families already permit normalized numerator
diameter exceeding `X` in the actual large terminal regime. Thus the no-carry
lemma genuinely breaks and an unbounded carry parameter appears.

A finite scratch search of small row lengths found no cofactor survivor, but
that is not an all-scale theorem and is not promoted.

Therefore RL315 must NOT be "exclude exactly three active cuts".
Any return to RL312/RL313 should target a support-independent full-D theorem.
