# Contributing and reviewing

The active question is E=empty for E={n>=2 : no iterate T^k(n) falls below n}, where T is the shortened 3n+1 map. All contributions must explain exactly how a result constrains this exceptional set.

For a new theorem include definitions, quantifiers, all hypotheses, a proof or reproducible finite verifier, and whether the result is novel, classical, conditional, finite, or externally cited. Seek counterexamples to proposed upgrades early.

Update research/PROOF_LEDGER.md when mathematical status changes and research/OPEN_GAPS.md when a logically necessary obligation changes. Research sessions go in research/sessions/; no compulsory per-session transport, session quotas, audits, synthetic progress percentages or promises of breakthroughs.

The preserved pre-2026-10-15 programme is under legacy/ and on the archive/pre-descent-gap-relaunch-2026-10-15 branch. Its frozen documents remain unchanged. Propose corrections to active interpretations in the active ledger, not by rewriting the archival source.
